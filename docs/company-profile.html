Company Profile

Who we are

Trishakti Apparel is a full-package (FOB) knit garment manufacturer in Gaidakot, Nepal. We produce private-label knitwear — t-shirts, polos, sweatshirts, hoodies and activewear — to our customers' own designs and tech packs, from fabric sourcing through to retail-ready, branded finishing. Founded in 2023, we build on a 33-year family fashion legacy (Trishakti Stores, est. 1991) and today supply 750+ retail and wholesale stores across Nepal alongside our growing export business.

We are a development partner, not just a contract sewer: with in-house Optitex CAD we take a sketch or reference to a graded, production-ready pattern — and we are candid about what we hold and what is in progress on compliance.

Capabilities

  • Design & development — production to your designs/spec charts; pattern & fit development from a sketch or reference.
  • In-house CAD & pattern making — Optitex for pattern making, marking, grading and on-site resizing; consistent fits, efficient markers, less waste.
  • Cut & sew production — 150+-machine knit line: t-shirts, polos, tanks, long-sleeve, sweatshirts, hoodies and activewear.
  • Private-label finishing — your labels, care/size/composition marking, hangtags and retail-ready packaging.
  • Quality control — AQL 2.5 — in-line and final inspection to AQL 2.5 (2.5 major / 4.0 minor) or your level; every defect logged digitally to operation and operator. Buyer-appointed 3rd-party inspection welcome.
  • Digital production tracking — factory ERP with real-time bundle scanning and live piece-count reconciliation from cutting to packing.
  • Fabric & trims sourcing — flexible global sourcing (primarily China), with duty management support.

Duty-free advantage
