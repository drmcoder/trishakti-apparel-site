# REX (Registered Exporter) — TEPC application checklist

Source: official TEPC (Trade & Export Promotion Centre, Pulchowk, Lalitpur) requirement
sheet (Nepali), translated + cross-checked against Trishakti's known documents.
**Confirm final specifics directly with TEPC — info@tepc.gov.np — do not treat this as legal advice.**

REX lets you self-certify origin (the "Statement on Origin") on duty-free EU/UK
shipments **over €6,000**. Under €6,000 you can already self-declare without REX.
See [[export-playbook]].

## B. The two things holding it up (from our export playbook)
1. **Pre-Application form (क):** on the system form, put the **13-digit EXIM code in the TIN field** — not the 9-digit PAN. A wrong TIN is a common rejection cause.
2. **Capacity figure:** submit your **correct current annual capacity**. TEPC's file figure is stale/low; your real capacity now exceeds the Rs 2 crore threshold. This ties to item **ट** — the capacity increase likely needs the **Department of Industry technical-committee recommendation** reflecting the new 150+-machine line.

## C. After REX is granted — ongoing obligation
- After each export **to an EU country**, submit **one copy of the Invoice (with the Statement on Origin)** back to TEPC. (Simple compliance step — keep a copy on file.)

## D. How TEPC issues it
They verify the application against the filing date, **may physically inspect** the factory (or the industries you list), then issue the REX number. So the factory being real, registered, and matching the declared capacity matters — all of which is true for Trishakti.

---

## Do-first order
1. **Get the Dept. of Industry technical-committee recommendation** for the enhanced capacity (item ट) — this is the piece most likely missing and it gates the capacity figure.
2. **Regenerate the Pre-Application** with EXIM in the TIN field + correct capacity (item क).
3. **Pull a current tax-clearance certificate** (item ञ).
4. **Write the stamped application letter** (item ख).
5. Photocopy the held certificates (ग, घ, ङ, च, ज, झ) — you already have these.
6. Submit the full set to TEPC, Pulchowk.

**In parallel (more urgent for small first orders):** register for the **Certificate of Origin**
at the Chitwan / Narayangarh Chamber of Commerce — that's what proves "Made in Nepal"
per shipment and is needed regardless of REX.
