# Factory video — one-hour phone shot list (26 Aug 2026)

Goal: the trust asset no competitor in our set has — real footage of a real floor. One phone,
one morning, landscape orientation, no music needed. Shoot everything in one session; the
clips feed GBP posts, LinkedIn, outreach emails, the gallery, and buyer replies for a year.

**Technical rules:** landscape (horizontal) · wipe the lens · morning light · walk slowly,
pan slower than feels natural · 15–40 seconds per clip · get verbal OK from anyone filmed
close-up · no copyrighted music playing in the background.

## The 12 shots, in shooting order

| # | Shot | How | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gate & signboard | Stand across the road; 10s static, then walk in | Proof of address — matches GBP/website |
| 2 | Floor wide pan | From entrance corner, one slow 30s pan of the whole line | The "150+ machines" claim, visible |
| 3 | Cutting table | Fabric lay + cutting in action, 20s | Capability proof |
| 4 | Follow the bundle | Walk one bundle from cutting to a sewing station, 40s | The "unscripted walkthrough" feel |
| 5 | Sewing close-up | Over-shoulder of an operator (with OK), hands + machine, 20s | Skill, real work |
| 6 | Line supervisor talking | 15s of normal instruction-giving (natural, not staged) | A managed floor |
| 7 | QC station | Measuring against spec sheet, defect tagging, 20s | AQL claim made visible |
| 8 | Barcode/ERP scan | One bundle scanned, screen visible, 15s | The tech differentiator |
| 9 | Finishing & pressing | 15s | Full-package proof |
| 10 | Packing & cartons | Folding, polybags, export cartons with markings, 20s | Export-readiness |
| 11 | Team photo moment | The floor, waving or just working, 10s | Warmth; LinkedIn gold |
| 12 | Founder piece-to-camera | Santosh at the floor's edge, 30s, script below | The named-human trust anchor |

**Founder script (30 seconds, English, imperfect is better than polished):**
> "Namaste — I'm Santosh, founder of Trishakti Apparel in Gaindakot, Nepal. This is our
> floor — 150+ machines, and a team that's 80% women. We make private-label knitwear for
> buyers in the UK, Europe, Canada and Japan, duty-free. If you're watching this deciding
> whether we're real: book a live video call and direct the camera yourself. We'll show you
> everything. Thank you."

## Editing (30 min, phone is fine)

- Cut A — **90-second site/GBP version:** shots 1→2→4→7→10→12.
- Cut B — **30-second outreach version:** 2→5→10→12 (attach to first-touch emails).
- Keep all raw clips — individual shots become future GBP/LinkedIn posts.
- Add captions (many buyers watch muted): "150+ machines · Gaindakot, Nepal", "MOQ 1,000
  pcs/style", "0% duty UK · EU · Canada · Japan".

Upload targets: GBP (video post), gallery page (ask the dev session to embed), LinkedIn
launch post, YouTube (unlisted is fine — gives an embeddable link for emails).
