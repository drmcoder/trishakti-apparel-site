## Method & data limits
- **ImportYeti and Panjiva both block automated fetch (HTTP 403).** All customs relationships above
  were recovered from **Google search snippets** of those pages (which surface each Nepal exporter's
  "top customer"). This yields only the single top consignee per exporter, not full importer lists —
  a paid ImportYeti/Panjiva login would expand each exporter into its complete US-buyer list.
- Free customs data = **US sea shipments only**: no air, no parcel, **no EU/UK/CA/AU/JP importer
  records**. Most of the strongest names above (UK/JP/AU brands) therefore come from the
  **public "Made in Nepal" brand method**, not customs data.
- **No shipment figures fabricated.** Where volume wasn't visible in a snippet, none is stated.
- Excluded per instructions (already on our list): People Tree, Aiayu, Tree of Life, Ganesh Himal
  Trading, Ark Fair Trade, Gecko Gearz, Nomads, Mata Traders, Surya, Namaste-UK, Komodo, Mantra.
  (Encountered Ark Fair Trade, Ganesh Himal, Surya, Tree of Life again in results — correctly dropped.)
- Excluded as non-buyers / Nepal-based makers (they ARE the supply side, not customers): Dinadi,
  Kasa Fashion, Ekadesma, DIMAICHA, Lakhey, Kathmandu Clothing, Clothing in Nepal, Himalayan
  Handmades, Nepal Art Shop, Garments Nepal, LOGO (madeinnepal), Legendary Fashion, Wings2Fashion.
- Excluded — verified NOT Nepal-sourced: prAna, Toad&Co, Indigenous Designs (Peru), Maggie's
  Organics (Nicaragua/Costa Rica/NC), **Yala / Yala Designs (made in China, 18-yr China team)**.
